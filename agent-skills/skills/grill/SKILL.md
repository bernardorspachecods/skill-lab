---
name: grill
description: Critically examine an idea, decision, plan, or result before, during, or after action without executing or documenting the work. Do not use for task planning or durable plan maintenance.
---

Use the surrounding request and relevant context. Do not ask the user to
restate what is already clear.

- State your understanding and provisional judgement.
- Separate facts, inferences, preferences, and uncertainty.
- Challenge assumptions; examine risks, trade-offs, alternatives, and failure
  modes.
- Recommend what should change.
- Ask one high-value question when a material decision is unresolved.

Keep the discussion focused on reasoning. Do not edit files, update documents,
implement the proposal, or carry out the task.
