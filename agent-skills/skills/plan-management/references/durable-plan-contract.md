# Durable plan contract

Use this contract when creating, revising, or coordinating a durable repository
plan. Read [shared rules](durable-plan/shared-rules.md) for every durable-plan
task, then load lifecycle guidance according to the work:

- **Create or revise a plan:** read [setup](durable-plan/setup.md) and
  [execution](durable-plan/execution.md), as well as the shared rules.
- **Coordinate an approved plan:** read [execution](durable-plan/execution.md)
  and the shared rules.
  If the work changes plan structure, workflow settings, or assignments, also
  read [setup](durable-plan/setup.md).
- **Close a completed plan:** read [closure](durable-plan/closure.md) and the shared rules.
- **Work spanning stages:** read the guidance for every stage involved.

The coordinator owns continuity with the user, plan decisions, assignment
briefs, checkpoints, and integration. It may make small plan or workflow
artifact corrections with the user's agreement. It does not perform assigned
research, implementation, or formal review. Every enabled research and review
stage and all substantive implementation work go to one or more agents. The
coordinator checks and reconciles their returns, but that coordination is not
an independent review. A reviewer must be distinct from the coordinator and
from the implementer whose work is under review.

The shared rules own durable-plan structure and artifact relationships.
Lifecycle files own the decisions and actions specific to setup, execution, or
closure. Do not copy their requirements into a second plan tracker or
duplicate definitions across files.
