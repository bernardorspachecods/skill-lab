# Audit

## Question

How can an LLM plan useful web search paths and identify valuable sites or
source types for a bounded research question, while distinguishing discovery
leads, source quality, and support for exact claims?

## Claims and evidence

- C1 — A search plan can map likely source roles and records from the question
  rather than rely on generic domain prestige. Find relevant methods or
  evaluations and limits.
- C2 — Controlled query expansion, citation paths, and disconfirming searches
  can help identify relevant evidence and alternatives. Determine when each
  path adds value.
- C3 — Source accountability, provenance, method, currency, and incentives
  inform source quality but do not prove that a source supports a given claim.
  Verify the evidence and exact boundaries.

All claims are pending. Do not assume a fixed list of valuable sites applies
across topics.

## Sources

Seek primary search-system documentation or evaluations where relevant,
research on retrieval/search agents, and accountable domain-specific sources
that demonstrate source mapping. Record dates, versions, independence, and
limitations.

## Conflicts and limitations

Search interfaces and indexing change. Retrieval results depend on query,
domain, and access. A source can be authoritative for one kind of claim and
unsuitable for another; do not collapse those distinctions.

## Validation

For each finding, identify the query or path and the evidence it surfaced.
Distinguish source quality from claim support and report when no suitable
source or independent corroboration was found.
