# Audit

## Question

What workflow makes an LLM extract relevant evidence from inspected sources
and turn it into direct, supported findings rather than a collection of source
summaries?

## Claims and evidence

- C1 — Explicit extraction targets tied to research questions or claims can
  improve the relevance of synthesis. Investigate what evidence supports this
  and under what conditions.
- C2 — Recording what a passage or dataset establishes and does not establish
  can prevent overclaiming. Seek methods or evaluation evidence and limits.
- C3 — Separating direct evidence, synthesis, inference, and recommendations
  can make research answers clearer and better calibrated. Verify the basis
  and avoid presenting a writing convention as a measured effect without
  support.

All claims are pending. Identify which are empirical and which are design
recommendations.

## Sources

Prefer original evaluations, methods, or authoritative guidance about
evidence extraction, claim verification, and synthesis by LLMs. Inspect the
underlying sources and methods; generated summaries are not supporting
evidence.

## Conflicts and limitations

Evaluations may use narrow datasets or benchmark tasks and may not predict
real-world research. Preserve task conditions and avoid generalized claims
about all LLM research.

## Validation

Trace each retained finding to a claim and exact source material. State what
the evidence supports, uncertainty, independence, and material limitations.
