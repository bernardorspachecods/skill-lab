## Review assignment

After the executor completes and the coordinator reconciles this subplan,
independently assess the changed source and search guidance in
`agent-skills/skills/research/SKILL.md`, using the target subplan's Outcome to
identify the change. Check fit to the brief, research audit, and completion
criteria. Confirm source-role mapping is question-led, search paths are
purposeful, and source quality remains distinct from claim support. Do not
edit the skill or other targets. Independently verify material source
descriptions and evidence-to-claim links in `LR-03.RES-04.audit.md`, including

## Conclusions

**Pass.** The revised `Workflow → 2. Plan and discover` guidance meets the
SP-03 brief and its completion criteria. Source roles are mapped from each
prioritized material question; the examples are explicitly non-exhaustive and
are not framed as a prestige ranking. Search expansions are limited,
purposeful, and traceable to the question or a relevant record. The guidance
includes citation and record-following paths, disconfirmation, and checks that
keep discovery candidates distinct from evidence and source quality distinct
from exact claim support.

The guidance builds on SP-02's prioritized questions, materiality, and
task-sensitive evidence adequacy, without replacing its scope or stopping
rules. Audit claims remain within their cited methods and benchmark contexts;
the audit explicitly limits generalization to arbitrary LLM web research.
No required corrections remain.

## Evidence

### Skill against the assignment

- `Workflow → 2. Plan and discover` first says to use prioritized questions to
  determine evidence needs and search paths. It directs the researcher to map
  source roles and record types per material question, then identify
  accountable producers, useful sites or collections, and domain terms. This
  implements question-led discovery and carries forward SP-02's prioritization
  and evidence-adequacy judgment.
- Examples connect source roles to the kind of fact sought (governing rule,
  original dataset or study, registry, original reporting). They are expressly
  “examples, not a fixed hierarchy or site whitelist”; the map is revised as
  searches reveal useful records or unproductive routes. This avoids a generic
  list of prestigious sites.
- For standard and high-stakes work, the four passes require a baseline query,
  a small set of purposeful variants with a stated reason, citation/reference
  and other record paths when likely useful, and disconfirmation when it could
  change the answer. This makes expansion bounded and traceable and provides
  purposeful corroboration and contrary-evidence paths.
- The skill explicitly treats results as discovery leads, requires opening
  candidates and checking provenance, dates, and exact-claim support, and
  separately retains source-quality assessment in Workflow 3. It says rank,
  official status, and other surface signals do not prove claim support.
  Workflow 3 retains the distinctions among origin, primary, and authoritative
  source roles. Thus the requested distinctions remain intact.
- The baseline workflow asks for sentinel checks only if reliable sentinel
  documents are known and instructs the researcher to record when such a check
  was unavailable. The audit's lack of an arbitrary-topic sentinel set is
  therefore not a criterion failure. The root plan's realistic example and
  integrated consumer-answer criteria are plan-wide checks, appropriately
  deferred to the later integration stage rather than required as an SP-03
  example.

### Independent audit verification

I opened the publisher, proceedings, or official records for S1–S8 (including
the full HTML for Jagerman et al. and the TARCiS correction record) and checked
the linked evidence claims against the audit:

- **E1, Stansfield et al.** The publisher record confirms the title, authors,
  journal, and 15 November 2016 date. Its text says appropriate websites
  depend on the precise research question, coverage, knowledge, and access;
  resource choices may change as new or unhelpful resources are found. It
  supports the stated question-led, revisable search plan and recordkeeping,
  within systematic-review website searching. The audit states this scope
  limit.
- **E2, PRISMA-S.** The publisher text supports naming databases, registries,
  online resources, and citation-search methods and reporting how they were
  used. It describes registries as a route to ongoing and potentially
  unpublished studies. The audit accurately frames this as systematic-review
  reporting guidance, not a universal requirement or proof that more sources
  ensure completeness.
- **E3, PaSa.** The ACL record confirms the agent's search, paper-reading, and
  reference-selection functions; the RealScholarQuery benchmark; and the
  reported 37.78% recall@20 and 39.90% recall@50 gains over the best
  Google-based baseline. The audit calls this a bounded academic-paper search
  evaluation and disclaims transfer to arbitrary web topics, retrieved-source
  quality, and exact claim support.
- **E4, Jagerman et al.** The arXiv HTML confirms the paper/version and
  benchmark results on MS MARCO and BEIR. Its limitations section says the
  study is sparse BM25 retrieval, uses specific prompt templates, and leaves
  dense retrieval for future work. The audit accurately confines the result
  to controlled retrieval conditions and does not claim open-web benefit.
- **E5, Query2doc.** ACL confirms the EMNLP 2023 citation and abstract's 3% to
  15% BM25 gains on MS MARCO/TREC DL and benefits for selected dense retrievers
  on in-domain and out-of-domain datasets. The audit carefully calls this one
  technique on benchmark sets and does not infer that every expansion term or
  research workflow is improved.
- **E6, TARCiS.** The BMJ record confirms the four-round expert Delphi with 27
  experts, the supplementary nature and variable yield of citation searching,
  the recommendation against standalone citation searching for completeness
  of recall, and reporting guidance for seeds, direction, dates, iterations,
  and stopping. The correction notice is recorded; the audit does not treat
  this systematic-review guidance as universally effective ordinary-web
  research.
- **E7, Google Search Central.** The official page describes query-sensitive
  freshness systems, link analysis/PageRank, neural matching, and original
  content systems. It is accurately treated as Google's own description of
  its ranking system only; the audit draws no source-quality score or claim
  about other search engines from it.
- **E8, Rashkin et al.** ACL confirms the AIS framework's requirement to verify
  external-world generation against an independent provided source and its
  validation on conversational QA, summarization, and table-to-text datasets.
  The audit uses this to distinguish source-to-output attribution from source
  discovery, while explicitly disclaiming source truth and general source
  quality.

The evidence-to-claim map is coherent: C1 uses E1–E3; C2 uses E4–E7; and C3
uses E1, E3, and E8 while disclosing that several quality dimensions are
methodological synthesis rather than separately validated predictors. Source
and evidence roles, overlap among systematic-review guidance and query
expansion papers, and benchmark transfer limits are stated. No material
misdescription or unsupported transfer was found in the inspected records.

## Uncertainties

No material uncertainty affects the SP-03 verdict. As the audit notes, the
search-method evidence is largely grounded in systematic-review work, and the
retrieval evaluations do not establish efficacy for full LLM web research
across arbitrary topics. The audit appropriately marks that transfer as
limited and describes its search paths as bounded rather than a recall
measurement. This review verifies the cited records and their reported
contents; it does not independently reproduce the benchmark experiments or
re-run the audit's search process.

## Recommendations

**Required corrections:** None.

**Optional advice:** None. The audit already reports a useful scope limitation
for the unavailable universal sentinel set, and the skill gives a matching
instruction to record when a reliable sentinel check is unavailable.
