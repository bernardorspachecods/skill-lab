# LR-05.SP-03

## Objective

Assess how outcomes of AI information loops are evaluated, what conditions
support their effectiveness, and what recurring limitations or failure modes
the evidence identifies.

## Scope

Build on the reviewed mechanism map and application audit in S1–S2. Reuse the
case evidence rather than repeating the application survey. Examine evaluation designs,
outcome measures, feedback quality and delay, proxy alignment, independence of
checks, and evidence about stability across iterations or contexts. Search for
material benefits, costs, and failures, including reinforcing errors,
optimizing a proxy instead of the goal, data or evaluator contamination,
drift, oscillation, reward hacking, overcorrection, and human-review burden
where those terms or phenomena are relevant in sources. These are prompts for
discovery, not assumed findings.

Keep source-reported findings separate from synthesis. Include disconfirming,
null, or adverse evidence and characterize whether it is empirical, theoretical,
or practitioner guidance. Do not combine unlike measures into a single
effectiveness claim.

## Output

Complete `LR-05.RES-03.audit.md` as a standard-rigor cross-cutting evidence
record on evaluation, conditions, benefits, costs, and failure modes. Link
claims to specific source evidence and preserve conflicts, limitations, and
search paths in `LR-05.RES-03.working.md`.

## Sequence

1. **S1 — Compare evaluation and failure evidence**
   - **Action:** Follow `$research` standard rigor, starting from the reviewed
     application evidence and seeking independent measures, boundary
     conditions, and refutations that could change the assessment.
   - **Output:** The completed audit and retained discovery log.
   - **Exit check:** Measures and claims are traceable; empirical results are
     distinguished from proposed mechanisms; limits and conflicts are
     assessed; the review is reconciled and the user checkpoint is approved.

## Completion criteria

- Measures are assessed for what they establish and whether they represent
  the intended outcome or only a proxy.
- Failure modes and benefits are reported only when supported and are
  calibrated to their evidence type and scope.
- Relevant null, mixed, adverse, and qualifying findings are represented;
  sources are not counted as independent when they share underlying evidence.
- The audit states material unknowns and a reasoned stopping point and passes
  independent review.

## Outcome

Research and independent review are complete and reconciled. The user approved
the S3 checkpoint on 2026-09-30. S3 is complete.
