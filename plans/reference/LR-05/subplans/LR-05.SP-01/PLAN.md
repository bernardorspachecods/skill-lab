# LR-05.SP-01

## Objective

Map the range of terms and mechanisms used for recurring feedback or revision
cycles involving AI and information work, without assuming “loop” has one
settled meaning.

## Scope

Start with the user's term “automated loops” and discover adjacent vocabulary
and uses. Seed searches may include feedback and closed-loop systems, iterative
generation and revision, self-refinement, evaluator-optimizer patterns,
human feedback, agent action-observation cycles, data or model improvement,
retrieval feedback, monitoring, and related terms. These are discovery leads,
not a fixed taxonomy or exclusion list. Search across technical, human, and
organizational settings where AI systems or AI-assisted work handle
information.

Require a direct connection to AI handling, producing, checking, retrieving,
learning from, monitoring, or governing information. Keep looser connections
visible as adjacent cases, not as core loop categories.

For each candidate mechanism, record what repeats; what state, output, or
information is observed; who or what supplies the signal; what changes next;
the objective or measure; the autonomy and human role; and the source's own
terminology. Keep systems that do not fit the provisional loop lens visible as
adjacent or disputed cases. Do not claim exhaustive coverage.

## Output

Complete `LR-05.RES-01.audit.md` with a standard-rigor evidence record: a
candidate mechanism map, term families and source-supported definitions,
linked claim/evidence entries, a source ledger, disputes, gaps, and a reasoned
coverage/stop account. Preserve exact search paths and material term changes
in `LR-05.RES-01.working.md`.

## Sequence

1. **S1 — Discover and classify candidate mechanisms**
   - **Action:** Follow `$research` standard rigor. Search broadly from the
     user's language, inspect multiple source roles, and expand terms from
     relevant records. Compare recurring structure and feedback targets;
     retain ambiguity and disconfirming examples.
   - **Output:** The completed audit and retained discovery log.
   - **Exit check:** Mechanism claims link to inspected sources; classification
     dimensions are explicit; alternatives and scope limits are recorded; the
     independent assessment is reconciled and the user checkpoint is approved.

## Completion criteria

- The map distinguishes mechanisms by the cycle and feedback signal they use,
  not only by labels or products.
- Terms, definitions, and classifications are attributed to sources or
  marked as the researcher's synthesis.
- The search records broad vocabulary discovery without asserting universal
  categories or complete recall.
- The six specialist areas raised during review (robotics/control, scientific
  workflows, labeling operations, online experiments, AIOps, and AI software
  engineering) receive a bounded coverage probe and are classified as a new
  mechanism, an instance of the map, or adjacent/outside scope with evidence.
- Material source disagreements, adjacent cases, gaps, and stopping rationale
  are visible and reviewed.
