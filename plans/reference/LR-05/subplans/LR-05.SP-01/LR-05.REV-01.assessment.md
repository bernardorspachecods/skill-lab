# Assessment

## Conclusions

**Result: the required correction is complete; no further required corrections
remain.** The researcher added a bounded query and evidence path for each of
the six named coverage areas, classified each against the map, updated the map
for adaptive experimentation, linked the new evidence to the existing claims,
and revised the stopping account. The resulting coverage statement stays
bounded: it explains the sampled classifications without claiming complete
field coverage.

The original audit strengths also remain: the map compares cycles by
mechanism, labels the dimensions as synthesis, and avoids universal taxonomy
or efficacy claims. The ledger distinguishes source quality, exact claim
support, and independence/overlap, and the audit preserves material
terminology disputes, failure cases, and limitations.

## Evidence

- **Scope and distinction quality — substantially met.** The question and
  boundary are explicit (audit, “Question and boundary”); the map compares
  repeat unit, observed state/content, signal, changed object, objective,
  autonomy, and human role, and labels these dimensions as researcher
  synthesis (audit, “Candidate mechanism map,” C10). It includes generation,
  tool/environment interaction, human-led learning, preference alignment,
  retrieval, recommenders, deployment monitoring, and governance. This
  satisfies the requirement to distinguish cycles by mechanism rather than
  label alone.
- **Evidence and source handling — met for the mapped examples.** C1–C10 link
  to E entries; entries identify source locations and specify what each source
  does and does not establish. The ledger separately assesses provenance and
  source role/quality, claim relevance, and independence/overlap (audit,
  “Evidence entries” and “Sources”). In particular, it does not treat NIST's
  framework and report as independent corroboration (S9–S10), nor vendor
  pattern documentation as efficacy evidence (S8, S11, S13). The audit also
  marks S12's abstract-only inspection and S10's report-summary limitation.
- **Disputes and limitations — met.** The audit reports conflicting or
  unstable human-loop taxonomies, explains why lifecycle, architecture, and
  learning taxonomies cannot be collapsed, and includes counterexamples to
  “feedback always improves quality” (audit, “Conflicts and limitations” and
  “Validation”). Its caveats on proxy signals, autonomy, and source roles are
  material to the map.
- **Six-area coverage probe — met for the assigned bounded check.** The
  working log records Q6–Q11, with exact queries, purposes, inspected paths,
  candidate dispositions, and findings/classifications for robotics/control,
  scientific discovery, labeling operations, online/adaptive experiments,
  AIOps, and AI software engineering. The audit summarizes these in its
  “Bounded coverage probe classifications” table and gives supporting entries
  E14–E19. Robotics is adjacent; scientific discovery, document labeling, and
  code self-debugging are instances of existing families; adaptive
  experimentation is added as a useful named subtype; and the inspected AIOps
  source does not establish a recurring closed cycle, so AIOps remains
  adjacent unless a specific case supplies that evidence.
- **Map, evidence IDs, and stopping rationale — coherent and bounded.** The
  adaptive-experimentation row and term-family note are supported by E17/S17;
  C1, C2, C4, C7, and C10 link relevant examples to E14–E19. Every new E14–E19
  points to a corresponding S14–S19 ledger entry. The ledger records
  provenance, role/quality, use, dates where available, and independence
  limitations. The revised rationale enumerates the six probe outcomes, says
  what changed, and limits the result to this probe rather than complete recall
  (audit, “Validation”; working log, “Cross-probe scope result”).
- **Source and inference limits are visible.** Several probe sources are
  abstract-level only (E14, E15, E18); their conclusions are accordingly
  narrow. E18 does not infer absence of AIOps loops from the abstract; it
  classifies only the inspected case as insufficient to establish closure.
  E17's distinction between experimentation and recommendation is marked as
  the audit's synthesis. Source quality, claim fit, and independence remain
  separately stated in the ledger.

## Uncertainties

This follow-up review checked the recorded query paths, audit classifications,
claim/evidence links, and source-ledger entries; it did not rerun every web
search or independently inspect each linked source. One record per named area
is sufficient to resolve whether the correction was documented and whether
its claims are bounded, but it does not establish the prevalence or full
taxonomy of any area. The audit acknowledges this limit. A deeper AIOps or
robotics survey could surface additional closed-cycle cases, but the current
text does not claim that such cases do not exist.
