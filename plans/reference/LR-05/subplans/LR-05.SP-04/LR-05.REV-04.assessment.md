The S1–S3 links resolve to the sibling audit paths and the cited S1 C1–C10, S2 C1–C9/E1–E12, and S3 C1–C8/E1–E8/E12–E14 references correspond to the audits' claim/evidence records.

The no-change strategy is not described as proven optimal; its residual risk is an unobserved gap. It is explicitly conditional and unauthorized in this subplan.

Proposed independent measures include blinded reviewer findings, residual defects, correction acceptance, downstream task/user outcomes, and time/compute, rather than a model's own pass score. These are proposed measures, not falsely reported as collected.
- **Recommendation calibration and boundaries:** The recommendation is to make no skill-level or active-workflow change now and consider a single low-risk pilot only after a recurring consequential gap is observed and the user agrees on baseline, measure, and stop condition. It says the external evidence cannot predict local effect. The Outcome states no new external research, skill edit, or active workflow change occurred.
