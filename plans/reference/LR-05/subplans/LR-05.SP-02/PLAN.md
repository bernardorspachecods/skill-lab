# LR-05.SP-02

## Objective

Determine where the loop mechanisms found in S1 are applied in AI information
work and what evidence exists about their intended and observed outcomes.

## Scope

Use S1's reviewed mechanism map as a search guide, not a closed list. Search across
research, deployed systems, and human or organizational workflows where
appropriate. Record applications across the information lifecycle, such as
generation, retrieval or grounding, checking and revision, learning from
feedback, monitoring, and governance when sources support those connections.

For each material case, identify the problem and setting, loop mechanism,
feedback source, what changes over cycles, intended outcome, measured outcome,
comparison or evaluation design, evidence type, and limitations. Record enough
detail to interpret each application; S3 owns cross-case assessment of measure
validity and recurring failure conditions. Separate documented capability and
illustrative examples from measured effectiveness. Include negative, null, and
mixed results; do not generalize from a product description, benchmark, or
single deployment beyond its scope.

## Output

Complete `LR-05.RES-02.audit.md` as a standard-rigor evidence record comparing
applications and outcomes by mechanism and context, with claim/evidence links,
source provenance, conflicts, and uncertainty. Keep exact searches and
material scope changes in `LR-05.RES-02.working.md`.

## Sequence

1. **S1 — Locate and compare applications**
   - **Action:** Follow `$research` standard rigor, using the reviewed S1 map
     and relevant source-specific terms to find distinct application settings
     and evaluate their evidence.
   - **Output:** The completed audit and retained discovery log.
   - **Exit check:** Each material application claim has evidence suited to
     the claim; capability is separated from effect; outcomes, context,
     uncertainty, and the independent review are reconciled; the user
     checkpoint is approved.

## Completion criteria

- Applications are organized around supported uses and information tasks,
  rather than a presumed exhaustive list of sectors.
- Evidence type and strength are explicit, including differences between
  conceptual proposals, vendor claims, controlled evaluations, and deployments.
- Intended targets and measured outcomes are not conflated; sources are
  assessed for provenance and independence.
- Contrary evidence, transfer limits, unmeasured outcomes, and search limits
  are visible and reviewed.
