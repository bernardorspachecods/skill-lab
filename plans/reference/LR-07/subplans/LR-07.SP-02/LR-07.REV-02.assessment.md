# Assessment

## Conclusions

**Review result: pass.** The KNOW-02 supplement stays within the approved
scope, integrates vendor material into existing sections, and preserves the
empirical synthesis and its transfer limits. The S2 audit distinguishes its
record-level sufficiency review from source-level verification and makes the
no-search decision only for the bounded integration candidates. I found no
required correction.

## Evidence

- **Scope and placement — pass.** KNOW-02 retains its existing section and
  case headings (see `knowledge/KNOW-02.md`, lines 13–192); the vendor
  additions are paragraphs within “How the cycle works,” the existing
  multi-step supervision case, and existing configuration subsections (lines
  40, 76, 141, and 147). No standalone vendor section or new user-need case
  was added. The browser-intervention case remains grounded only in the
  CowPilot study and its existing limits (lines 82–86); no vendor pause,
  takeover, or handoff example is transferred into it.
- **New claims and evidence fit — pass.** The cycle illustration at line 40
  maps to AUD-04 E19 (Anthropic goal condition/turn cap), E5–E6 (OpenAI
  validation feedback and bounded repair), and E101/E107 (Microsoft evaluator
  feedback, iteration bound, and caveats). I reopened the current official
  pages: Anthropic specifies goal achievement or a maximum number of turns
  and says to apply patterns selectively ([AUD-04 S1/E19, S1/E2]); OpenAI's
  Codex example describes validation-pass, attempt-cap, unchanged-delta, and
  human-review stop conditions ([AUD-04 S3/E6]); Microsoft says looping is
  experimental, defaults to 10 invocations/runs, and is not available in Go
  ([AUD-04 S9/E101, E107]). The text calls these product examples, not
  outcome evidence.
- **Case and configuration additions — pass with a bounded placement note.**
  The multi-step supervision paragraph (KNOW-02 line 76) labels Microsoft
  evaluator repetition and Azure maker-checker as implementation examples,
  explicitly says they do not corroborate the simulation's timing result,
  and makes no checkpoint-effect claim. The source contexts are automated
  agent/framework patterns, not evidence of how users typically supervise
  agents; the wording preserves that distinction. The configuration additions
  at lines 141 and 147 label vendor advice and caps as product-specific
  choices, not proven best practice or optimal thresholds. The official
  Microsoft pages support the stated experimental/default-cap/Go caveat and
  Azure maker-checker feedback, criteria, iteration-cap, and fallback
  description ([AUD-04 S9/E101, E107; S11/E103, E108]).
- **No transfer of vendor effects or typical behavior — pass.** KNOW-02 adds
  no vendor-reported metrics to “What the evidence supports,” no claim that
  product examples represent typical user practice, and no assertion that
  any configuration improves outcomes. AUD-05's integration map explicitly
  records these omissions and the excluded browser-case route (C3/C5 and
  “Integration map and source-type guardrails”).
- **Empirical baseline — pass.** I compared the full KNOW-02 text with the
  approved LR-06 result at

  do not change their stated evidentiary meaning.
- **No-search conclusion and phase-5 record — pass, bounded to the stated
  question.** AUD-05 C1–C5 and its phase-2 coverage summary identify no
