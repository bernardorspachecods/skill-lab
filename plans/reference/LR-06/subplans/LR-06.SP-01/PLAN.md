# LR-06.SP-01

## Objective

Map how feedback loops are named and structured when people use AI agents in
information workflows, and establish vocabulary for the later case review.

## Scope

Start from the user's term “loops” and search adjacent terminology used in
agentic workflows, such as iterative generation and revision, self-feedback,
tool/action/observation cycles, evaluator-optimizer, retrieval feedback,
human-agent interaction, verification and correction, planning and replanning,
and related terms found in sources. These are discovery leads, not a fixed
taxonomy.

For each candidate mechanism, record the workflow task; what repeats; what
state, output, or evidence is observed; who or what supplies the signal; what
changes on the next cycle; how iteration stops; the agent's autonomy; the
person's role; and the intended or measured outcome. Require a direct
connection to a person using an AI agent to do information work. Keep model
training, platform-level recommendation dynamics, and organization-wide
monitoring as adjacent cases unless a source connects them directly to the
user-agent workflow.

Do not claim a complete taxonomy or literature coverage. Distinguish source
terminology from the researcher's analytic grouping.

## Output

Complete `LR-06.RES-01.audit.md` with a standard-rigor evidence record,
workflow-focused mechanism map, source-supported terms, linked claims,
source ledger, boundaries, and search limits. Preserve exact searches and
material scope changes in `LR-06.RES-01.working.md`.

## Sequence

1. **S1 — Discover workflow loop mechanisms**
   - **Action:** Follow standard `$research` rigor. Search broadly from the
     user's workflow framing; expand terms from relevant sources; probe
     multiple user task settings without treating the seed list as exhaustive.
   - **Output:** Completed audit and retained discovery log.
   - **Exit check:** Mechanism claims link to inspected sources; direct and
     adjacent cases are distinguishable; terminology, classification limits,
     search paths, and independent review are reconciled.

## Completion criteria

- The map is organized around tasks and feedback mechanics in user-agent
  workflows, not around AI system sectors or product categories.
- Each mechanism records signal, change, termination, autonomy, and human
  role where evidence supports those details.
- Source terminology, researcher synthesis, adjacent cases, and coverage
  limits are explicit.
- The audit and working log follow standard `$research` rigor, pass
  independent review, and are reconciled by the coordinator before S2 starts.
