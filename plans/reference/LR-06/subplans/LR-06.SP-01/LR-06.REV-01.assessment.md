It explicitly distinguishes direct user-agent evidence from adjacent code-completion, fictional health scenarios, creative generation, and agent-internal feedback (Audit, C5; E4–E8; Sources S4, S6, S8; Conflicts and limitations). It also avoids claiming exhaustive coverage.
- **Evidence locations and claim fit:** E1–E9 provide source URLs/versions and section, page, or line locations, and explain what each source supports and does not establish. I spot-checked the cited primary-source locations and material results for the coding-agent study (E1), deep-research study (E2), data-analysis paper (E3), health-information study (E4), checkpoint study (E5), code-completion study (E6), and the repository record for the writing study (E9). The reviewed passages support the audit's stated distinctions and caveats.
- **Reported results versus synthesis:** The audit separates “What this establishes” from “Does not establish,” labels cross-study groupings as synthesis, and identifies the coding and checkpoint results as context-bound rather than universal effects (C2, C4, C6; E1, E5; Validation; Conflicts and limitations). The 38%/83% coding success rates are accurately reported as descriptive associations, not causal estimates. The checkpoint study's 81% preference and 13.54% completion-time reduction are correctly bounded to its simulated evaluation.
- **Evidence type, provenance, and corroboration:** The source ledger describes study type, sample/context, access limits, and claim fit. It correctly treats E1/E7 and E8 as one underlying source cluster, not independent confirmation, and states that the remaining studies use distinct author teams/samples (Sources; Validation).
- **Coverage and stop rationale:** The audit documents multiple search routes, source inspection and citation-chaining limits, thin planning coverage, and the abstract-only access for E9. Its stop statement is explicitly an adequacy judgment for this bounded map, not a recall or exhaustiveness claim (Additional inspected candidates; Conflicts and limitations; Validation). The working log provides the exact search queries and navigation routes that contextualize that rationale.
