# Assessment

## Conclusions

**Initial review outcome: correction required; re-review outcome: correction satisfied.** The audit substantially meets the cross-case scope: it distinguishes direct workflow evidence from adjacent/model-internal evidence, separates source results from transfer synthesis, accounts for positive/mixed/adverse/null-like evidence, and bounds its search stop. The initial review identified a DRV measure-classification issue; the requested update now makes the feedback-signal distinction explicit (see Re-review below).

## Evidence

- **Measure independence — required correction.** In E8 and the mechanism map, the secure-and-correct outcome is described as independent of the model report, and the audit also notes that the same test oracle is used across variants ([audit, E8 and cross-case map, lines 55 and 69](../../../../knowledge/audit/KNOW-02.AUD-03.md#L55)). However, the primary DRV paper says each iteration reruns functional and exploit tests and proceeds to another repair when either check fails; success requires both checks to pass ([Cheng, §4.2.1–4.2.4, lines 424–441](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.23633#S4); see also [§5.1, lines 448–450](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.23633#S5)). Those tests are external to the model's own textual assessment, but they are also the loop's feedback signal and the final score. The plan requires the audit to state whether each measure is independent of the loop's own feedback signal, so this distinction must be explicit in E8, the case map, and the outcome-independence summary ([audit, lines 90–92](../../../../knowledge/audit/KNOW-02.AUD-03.md#L90)).
- **Peer-review RCT spot-check — supported.** E4 accurately reports the intervention and endpoints. The primary report describes submission-level randomization, optional reviewer edits, and feedback arriving one hour after submission ([Thakkar et al., §§2.1–2.2, lines 60–85](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.09737#S2)). It reports 18,946 reviews receiving feedback and 26.6% updating; the 89% incorporation estimate is LLM-classified and applies to updaters, while the 89/100 blinded preference result is from a selected subset conditioned on updating and incorporation ([§§3.1–3.2, lines 154–180](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.09737#S3)). The audit correctly preserves the selection caveat, subjective quality boundary, and null average-score-change endpoint ([audit, C2 and E4, lines 29 and 47](../../../../knowledge/audit/KNOW-02.AUD-03.md#L29)).
- **DRV benchmark spot-check — reported outcomes supported, with the classification issue above.** Primary methods confirm functional tests check preserved behavior and exploit tests check whether the targeted vulnerability remains reproducible; both must pass for verified success, and failure leads to another iteration up to the cap ([Cheng, §4.2.1–4.2.4, lines 424–441](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.23633#S4)). Results support the audit's account that project-level repair can fail to converge while narrower requirement/file scopes are more stable, with incomplete repair and regressions among failure modes ([§5.1, lines 455–459](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.23633#S5); [§5.3, lines 691–715](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.23633#S5)). The paper also identifies oracle coverage and bounded iteration as validity limits ([§7.1–7.2, lines 742–748](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.23633#S7)). The audit appropriately labels this as adjacent autonomous code repair, not human-agent workflow evidence, and states that runtime/human effort are unmeasured ([audit, E8 and map, lines 55 and 69](../../../../knowledge/audit/KNOW-02.AUD-03.md#L55)).
- **Other completion criteria.** The claim matrix links each finding to evidence, including prior S1/S2 material without recounting those studies as independent confirmations ([audit, lines 16–23 and source table, lines 81–83](../../../../knowledge/audit/KNOW-02.AUD-03.md#L16)). Positive, null/mixed, adverse, and unlocated categories are explicitly differentiated; the no-null stopping-policy statement is bounded to the searched routes ([audit, lines 87–94](../../../../knowledge/audit/KNOW-02.AUD-03.md#L87)). The conclusions also distinguish directly tested conditions from cross-case inference and state a bounded stop rationale ([audit, lines 98–102](../../../../knowledge/audit/KNOW-02.AUD-03.md#L98)).

## Uncertainties

- The DRV paper's tests provide an external, executable oracle relative to model-generated critique, but the review cannot characterize its outcome as independent of the loop's feedback signal because those same tests guide iteration. The audit should preserve both senses of “independent.”
- I spot-checked the complete primary-source passages for the ICLR RCT and the core DRV methods/results/validity claims. Other source records were reviewed for internal fit and claim framing, not independently re-extracted in full during this review.

## Recommendations

### Initial required correction (resolved on re-review)

1. Clarify that E8's final test score is independent of the model's self-report, but dependent on/reuses the same functional and exploit-test signal used to drive later repairs. Reflect that classification in the cross-case mechanism map and the outcome-independence summary. Do not describe E8 as having an outcome independent of the loop's own feedback signal without that qualification.

### Optional advice

- None beyond the required measurement clarification. The audit already records the test-coverage, domain-transfer, resource-measurement, and bounded-search limitations relevant to this evidence.

## Re-review

**Outcome: required correction satisfied.** The updated E8 now states that functional and exploit tests are external to the model's self-report but also provide feedback to subsequent repair rounds and determine the final secure-and-correct score; it therefore explicitly says the outcome is not independent of the loop feedback signal ([audit, E8, line 55](../../../../knowledge/audit/KNOW-02.AUD-03.md#L55)). The cross-case map and outcome-independence summary repeat this distinction ([audit, lines 69 and 91](../../../../knowledge/audit/KNOW-02.AUD-03.md#L69)). The test-coverage limitation remains explicit in E8 and the outcome-independence summary: the oracle can miss defects ([audit, lines 55 and 91](../../../../knowledge/audit/KNOW-02.AUD-03.md#L55)). No further correction is required for the assigned re-review item.
